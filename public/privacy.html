Reading as a guest

Guest notes, bookmarks, annotations and preferences are stored in your browser. They are not automatically uploaded when you sign in. You may explicitly copy guest work into your account. Clearing browser data can remove device-only work; use the export controls to keep a backup.

Google sign-in and private sync

Google sign-in requests your basic identity, email and profile information to create and identify your account through Supabase. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive or other Google content. Signed-in workspace notes, marks, bookmarks and preferences are stored in your Supabase account and cached on your device. Database access policies restrict workspace rows to their account owner.

Google account information is used for authentication and account identification. The app does not use it for advertising, sell it, or send your private notes to an AI service. Google and Supabase process authentication information under their own privacy policies.

Separate annotation studio copies

Studio drafts are stored separately on your device for the current workspace. They do not sync to the reader or the cloud automatically. “Apply this page to reader” copies the current draft into your personal reader; signed-in reader changes can then sync. PDF, PNG and study-copy exports are generated in your browser.

Storage and external services

Signing out switches to guest mode but does not erase cached account copies from the device or delete cloud data. Browser storage is accessible to people with access to that browser profile. Vercel hosts the website; Supabase provides account and database services. These services may process technical request and security logs. Quran sources, recitation providers and embedded video services receive requests when you use their content. The site does not include an added advertising or analytics service.

Your choices

You can use the reader without signing in, export your workspace, clear page marks, and sign out. Export important work before clearing website storage. For account-data access or deletion requests, contact the site operator at blackchocot2@gmail.com. Account deletion is not currently a self-service button.
